## What is Acorn?

Acorn is a Reddit client for iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo, built by an independent developer. It isn't affiliated with Reddit.

## Do I need Pro to use Acorn?

No. Pro is only for customizing the app, not for normal use. Signing in, browsing, voting, commenting, posting, saving, searching and everything else you need day to day is free.

## What does Pro unlock?

Pro lets you make Acorn your own. It unlocks themes, fonts, custom gestures, default sorting, filters, your default feed and search tabs, video downloads, and more than two accounts.

Options that need Pro are dimmed in settings, and tapping one takes you to the Pro screen.

## What plans are there?

There's a monthly plan, a yearly plan and a one time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency. If a plan comes with a free trial, the button will say so.

Have an offer code? Tap Redeem on the Pro screen.

## I bought Pro but it's not showing up

Pro belongs to your Apple Account, not your Reddit account. It works with every Reddit account you add, and on every iPhone and iPad signed in to the same Apple Account.

If Pro isn't showing up, open the Pro screen and tap Restore.

## How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so we can't cancel them for you. Tap Manage on the Pro screen, or go to your subscriptions in the App Store.

## Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). Our [terms of use](/terms) have the details.

## Does Pro guarantee Acorn will keep working?

No. Pro supports Acorn's development, but Reddit controls how third party apps access it and can change or cut off access at any time. If that happens and we can't work around it, we'll stop selling Pro and let subscribers know so they can cancel. See our [terms of use](/terms).

## Why does Acorn show up as an Android app in my Reddit account?

Acorn talks to Reddit the same way Reddit's own Android app does. So in your Reddit account settings, and in any sign in emails from Reddit, Acorn shows up as an Android app. That's expected, and it's Acorn.

## Does Acorn see my password?

No. You sign in on Reddit's own login page, and Acorn never sees or stores your password.

## Can my Reddit account get in trouble for using Acorn?

Acorn isn't officially approved by Reddit, and Reddit may consider unofficial apps against its terms. That means there's always some risk, and you use Acorn at your own risk. See our [terms of use](/terms).

## Can I use more than one account?

Yes. You can add two accounts for free, and as many as you like with Pro.

## Can I browse without signing in?

No. Acorn needs a Reddit account to work.

## Do my settings sync between devices?

Yes. Your accounts, preferences and gestures sync between your devices through iCloud. Your read history and filters stay on the device they were made on.

## Are there ads?

No. Acorn doesn't show ads, and doesn't use analytics or tracking. Our [privacy policy](/privacy) has the details.

## Can I save images and videos?

Saving images is free. Downloading videos is part of Pro. Acorn only asks for access to your photo library when you save something.

## Can I translate posts?

Yes. Acorn uses Apple's built in translation to translate posts and comments.

## Does Acorn have push notifications?

Not right now. Open the notifications tab to see your inbox.

## Can I send messages or chat?

Not right now. Reddit chat and messages aren't supported.

## Is there an Android version?

No. Acorn is designed for iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo only.

## Is Acorn open source?

Yes. Acorn's source code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/alizahid/acorn) under the GPL 3.0 license.

## How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Come say hi on [Discord](https://discord.gg/sWzw5GU5RV). That's the best place for bugs, feature requests and questions.

## Can I try new features early?

Yes. Join the beta on [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/uKWP3MFB) to get new builds before they hit the App Store.

## How else can I support Acorn?

Pro is the best way. You can also [buy me a coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/acornblue), or tell a friend about Acorn.
