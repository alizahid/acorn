## Overview

Acorn is a Reddit client.

Acorn has no servers, accounts or databases of its own. We don't show ads, we don't use analytics or tracking, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone.

## Your Reddit account

You sign in on Reddit's own login page. Acorn never sees or stores your password.

After you sign in, Acorn keeps the session that Reddit issues so it can load your feed and act on your behalf. Everything you do in the app, like browsing, voting, posting, commenting, messaging and uploading media, goes directly from your device to Reddit and is covered by Reddit's [privacy policy](https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy).

## Data on your device

Your signed in accounts, preferences and gestures are stored on your device and synced between your devices through your iCloud account. Your read history, collapsed comments, sort orders and filters are stored only on your device. We have no access to any of this.

iCloud sync is provided by Apple and covered by Apple's [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Crash reporting

When something goes wrong, Acorn sends an error report to Sentry so we can fix it. Reports include the error, technical details about your device and app version, and your Reddit username. A very small sample of performance data is also collected. Refer to Sentry's [privacy policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/).

## Other services

Some media, like RedGIFs videos and GIPHY images, is loaded directly from the service that hosts it. Like any website, these services can see your IP address when your device connects to them.

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; the app only checks with the App Store whether you have an active subscription.

Translation uses Apple's built in translation feature.

App updates are delivered through Expo, whose servers receive standard request information when the app checks for updates. Refer to Expo's [privacy policy](https://expo.dev/privacy).

## Permissions

Acorn only asks for access to your photo library when you choose to save media or attach media to a post. It isn't used for anything else.

## Website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or collect any data.

## Children

Acorn isn't meant for children under 13, or the minimum age required by Reddit in your country.

## Your choices

You can remove an account by signing out, and remove all local data by deleting the app. If you'd like crash reports linked to your username deleted, reach out to us.
